# bestTime migration - mixed season + time-of-day text (proposals)

Proposal sheet. **Nothing in this sheet has been applied yet.** The released locations whose legacy `bestTime` mixes season and time-of-day text need splitting into `bestSeasonNote` (shown under the month strip) and `bestTimeOfDay` (its own card).

Rules used:
- The original wording is kept verbatim. Only the following changed: en/em dashes became hyphens, a leading "Within a day," was dropped, "year-round" was dropped from one time-of-day phrase (`hang-mua`), and a trailing ";" became ".".
- Sentences that name months or seasons go to `bestSeasonNote`; sentences about the time of day go to `bestTimeOfDay`.
- Where a sentence ties a time of day to a season (e.g. "Nov-Dec: catch sunset on the afternoon crossing"), the whole text stays in `bestSeasonNote` ("season only").
