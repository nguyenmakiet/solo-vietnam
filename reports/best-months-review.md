## Highest-priority manual review before migration

1. **Explicit contradictions** (REAL_CONFLICT, high confidence): `nho-que-river` (May in bestMonths but 'best avoided'; Jan-Feb missing), `pongour-waterfall` (two valid seasons, both cut), `hanoi-st-josephs-cathedral` (bestMonths doesn't match its own text), `tam-coc` (Jul missing, Sep-Oct unexplained), `yen-tu-mountain` (festival season excluded, Oct-Dec unexplained), `binh-lieu-border-mountains` (Jan missing).
2. **Closed location with a month strip**: `cat-ba-cannon-fort` (status `closed`; the seasonal text is about the Radio Tower). Decide how closed / seasonally-closed locations render `bestMonths` before the UI ships.
3. **Content claims to verify before they become user-facing**: `cat-cat-village` ('May - Sep warm, sunny' in Sapa's rainy season), `notre-dame-cathedral-saigon` (renovation status), `datanla-waterfall` and `phi-lieng-waterfall` (does the rainy season count as worth considering?).
4. **High-priority NEEDS_RESEARCH** (outdoor, season-sensitive, time-of-day-only text): beaches and islands (`bai-dai-cam-ranh-beach`, `dam-trau-beach`, `doi-nhai-beach`, `ham-tien-beach`, `six-senses-beach`, `sunworld-beach-cat-ba`, `ti-top-island`, `pirate-islands`), waterfalls / streams (`khe-van-waterfall`, `elephant-waterfall`, `pa-sy-waterfall`, `mooc-spring`, `dark-cave`), rice / field / grass landscapes whose month strip implies a harvest season (`don-village`, `kho-muong-village`, `khau-pha-pass`, `s-shape-rice-terraces`, `ta-pa-fields`, `vinh-trung-fields`, `masara-hill`).
5. **Owner policy decision**: for urban / indoor sites where the text never advises avoiding a season (`independence-palace`, the LOW-priority NEEDS_RESEARCH list), is year-round the default, or must each one be sourced? Deciding this once removes ~20 individual research tasks.

## Notes on SAFE (not re-reviewed)

SAFE = consistent with the regex reading of the text. Not re-reviewed per scope, but three SAFE rows use 'year-round' in a way worth a glance: `jade-emperor-pagoda` ('early morning year-round' is a time-of-day phrase), `vinpearl-cable-car` ('operates year-round' is a facility statement; Dec-Jan are unmentioned), `hospital-cave` ('Year-round (exterior only ... or skip entirely)'). The other 15 SAFE 'year-round' rows use 'Year-round;' as the recommendation itself.

Regex limitations seen in this review (worth fixing in the audit script before it becomes a CI gate): Tết / Christmas / lunar dates are not mapped to months; negative wording in a following sentence ('Nov-Apr ... is actually the rougher season') is missed; 'less ideal' and 'flooding' are not negative keywords; conditionals ('if visiting in summer') are read as recommendations; 'year-round' attached to a time of day is read as seasonal.
